John (Junseo) Heo is an undergraduate in Music Technology at Georgia Tech who comes to L42i from a professional audio background — installing and programming sound systems for hotels and retail spaces, and mixing live for churches around Atlanta. At L42i he installed the lab's 28-channel truss-mounted speaker array and configured its Dante network, including clock and PTP setup and switch design. His research interests center on immersive audio and interaction in virtual and augmented environments.
