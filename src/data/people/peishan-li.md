Peishan Li is a sound designer, music producer, pianist, and EDI performer. Her interests center on interactive and immersive music technologies, HCI, spatial audio, and human-centered approaches to understanding musical and technological experiences. Peishan is a first-year M.S. student in Music Technology at Georgia Tech and a member of the Lab for Interaction & Immersion (L42i), where she works with Prof. Henrik von Coler. She holds a B.M. in Electronic Production and Design from Berklee College of Music, with a background in sound design, electronic music production, game audio, audio programming, and interactive/VR musical systems.
