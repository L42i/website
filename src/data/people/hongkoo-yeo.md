Hongkoo (HK) Yeo is an architect and Music Technology Master’s student at the Georgia Institute of Technology exploring computational relationships between spatial form, sound, and perception. His research interests include spatial music composition, computational design, immersive interaction, and digital cultural heritage. At L42i, he investigates how architectural space, human movement, and sonic structure connect through interactive systems, with a particular interest in multisensory approaches to experiencing historical and cultural spaces. Hongkoo holds a Bachelor of Architecture from Korea University, Seoul and a Master of Architecture from Georgia Tech. He is a guitarist, bassist, music producer and DJ.
