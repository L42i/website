# 26-27 GA3D Contest

In the 26-27 academic year, the **GA3D** composition contest returns. After a successful first edition in 25-26, the contest is expanding: students from **Georgia Tech**, the **University of Georgia**, **Emory University** and **Georgia Southern University** are invited to submit electronic pieces of any style or genre that utilize Ambisonics 3D audio formats.
Selected finalists' pieces will be featured at a concert in Atlanta in late April 2027.

### Details:
- Open to currently-enrolled students at Georgia Tech, the University of Georgia, Emory University and Georgia Southern University.
- Pieces of **any style or genre** are welcome.
- Submissions must be in **AmbiX** format, **3rd to 7th order**.
- Prizes: a total of **$1000** in prize money will be awarded to the winners.
- Piece duration: approximately **5 to 12 minutes**. This is a guideline, not a hard cutoff.

### Important Dates:
- **March 15, 2027**: Deadline to submit entries.
- **Late April 2027**: Finalists' concert in Atlanta.

### Jury

An external jury will be announced.
