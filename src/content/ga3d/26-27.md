# 26-27 GA3D Contest

The Georgia 3D Composition Contest (GA3D) returns for 2026–27, celebrating immersive music and sound art in the region.
It started at Georgia Tech as the [24-25 GT3D Contest](/ga3d/24-25), grew into the [25-26 GA3D Contest](/ga3d/25-26), and this year opens to the whole state:

<div class="callout">
<span class="callout-label">Open call</span>

**Students from all colleges and universities in Georgia are invited to submit electronic pieces of any style or genre that use Ambisonics 3D audio formats.**

</div>

Submissions are due **March 15, 2027**, through a submission portal that will be announced ahead of time. Finalists' pieces will be performed at a concert in Atlanta in late April 2027, and the winners share **$1000** in prizes.
Find more details below.

### Details:
- Open to currently-enrolled students at **all colleges and universities in Georgia**.
- Pieces of **any style or genre** are welcome.
- Submissions must be in **AmbiX** format, **3rd to 7th order**.
- Pieces may be fixed-media, audiovisual, or include a live performance element, provided the composer arranges the performers. Every entry needs a complete AmbiX recording for the jury, and audiovisual submissions have include the video.
- Piece duration: approximately **5 to 12 minutes**. This is a guideline, not a hard cutoff.

### Important Dates:
- **March 15, 2027**: Deadline to submit entries. The submission portal will be announced ahead of time.
- **Late April 2027**: Finalists' concert in Atlanta.
