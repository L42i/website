# MËSH at CIC 2025

**2025** · Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
*Interactive installation / concert*

![](/assets/events/mesh-at-cic-2025/1.jpg)

L42i students Orlando Kenny and Henry Windish presented MËSH as an interactive installation and concert at CIC 2025 in Kuala Lumpur. The event included a performance of Henrik von Coler’s composition DISCOUNT by local musicians and demonstrated the lab’s approach to portable, participatory spatial-music systems.
