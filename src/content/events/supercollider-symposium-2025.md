# L42i at the SuperCollider Symposium 2025

**March 13-15, 2025** · Johns Hopkins University Bloomberg Center, Washington, D.C.
*Workshop / performance / paper presentation*

L42i contributed a multi-part program to the 2025 SuperCollider Symposium in Washington, D.C. The lab presented a student-led MËSH workshop, a 2CUBES performance using augmented-reality interfaces, and research on the SuperCollider-based spatial-audio performance system behind the work. Orlando Kenny led the workshop with support from Henry Windish and Ishaan Jagyasi; Hyunkyung Shin and Henrik von Coler presented 2CUBES.
