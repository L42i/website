# L42i at SinusTon 2024

**October 26-27, 2024** · Magdeburg, Germany
*Concert / sound installation*

![](/assets/events/l42i-at-sinuston-2024/1.jpg)
*Photo: Edgar Hartung*

The L42i research group presented a concert and sound installation at SinusTon in Magdeburg. The program featured the ARCube and the Nebula Euphone, bringing the lab’s augmented-reality and immersive performance work into an international festival setting.
