# Immersive Open Studios at Underground Atlanta

**Fall 2025** · Underground Atlanta, Atlanta, Georgia
*Student exhibition / open studio series*

![](/assets/events/immersive-open-studios-at-underground-atlanta-2025/1.jpg)
*Flyer.*

L42i and M.E.A.T. hosted a five-night Immersive Open Studios series at Underground Atlanta. The recurring public program presented student work in immersive media, spatial sound, and related creative technologies over five nights.
