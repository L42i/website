# BIKES at the Undergraduate Research Spring Symposium

**April 17, 2025** · Exhibition Hall, Georgia Tech, Atlanta, Georgia
*Poster / interactive demonstration*

![](/assets/events/bikes-urop-symposium-2025/1.jpg)
*Buzz, Georgia Tech's mascot, trying out BIKES.*

Undergraduate students presented a BIKES poster and demonstrated a working bicycle at Georgia Tech’s Undergraduate Research Spring Symposium. Visitors interacted with the touchscreen interface and the resulting sound, providing an early public test of the system before the four-bike mobile deployment. The demonstration followed the team’s poster session in the Exhibition Hall.
