# BIKES at SITE 2025

**September 27, 2025** · SITE Festival, The Goat Farm, Atlanta, Georgia
*Moving performance / interactive installation*

![](/assets/events/bikes-at-site-2025/1.jpg)

L42i presented BIKES: A Moving Network Instrument at SITE 2025. Four electric cargo bicycles equipped with computers, touchscreens, and loudspeakers formed a distributed instrument for experimental music and sound art. Visitors encountered the project across the Goat Farm as a mobile performance and interactive installation, with the student-developed system responding to the site’s changing public spaces and acoustics.
