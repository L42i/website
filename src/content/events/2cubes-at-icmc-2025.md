# 2CUBES at ICMC 2025

**June 8-14, 2025** · International Computer Music Conference, Boston, Massachusetts
*Performance / international conference*

2CUBES was presented at the 50th International Computer Music Conference in Boston. The performance connected double bass, modular synthesizer, and two augmented-reality interfaces, allowing the performers to shape and spatialize their own sound within a shared virtual scene. The event placed L42i’s AR performance research within an international computer-music program.
