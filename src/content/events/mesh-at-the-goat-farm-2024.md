# MËSH at The Goat Farm

**November 20, 2024** · The Goat Farm, Atlanta, Georgia
*Experimental network concert / immersive performance*

![](/assets/events/mesh-at-the-goat-farm-2024/1.jpg)

L42i presented MËSH as an experimental network concert at The Goat Farm. The event was led by Henrik von Coler with 22 Georgia Tech School of Music students and used a distributed network of electronic and electroacoustic performers to create a spatialized musical experience. The setup combined networked access points, local loudspeakers, multi-channel rendering, audience interaction, and projection within the venue.
