# BIKES at Industrial Design Launchpad

**May 1, 2025** · Georgia Tech College of Design, Atlanta, Georgia
*Interactive installation / design showcase*

![](/assets/events/bikes-launchpad-2025/1.jpg)

BIKES appeared at Launchpad, the Georgia Tech College of Design’s annual showcase, as an interactive quadraphonic installation using four networked audio bicycles. Visitors could use the touchscreen on each bike to alter the soundscape. The presentation tested the project’s portability, synchronization, and audience interaction in a busy public showcase environment.
