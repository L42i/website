# BIKES at the Inman Park Parade

**April 26, 2025** · Inman Park, Atlanta, Georgia
*Mobile performance / public parade*

![](/assets/events/bikes-at-the-inman-park-parade-2025/1.jpg)

The BIKES team presented all four audio-equipped bicycles in motion during the Inman Park Parade. Each bike contributed a distinct layer to a distributed musical performance while the riders moved through the public route. The activation served as the first full-system field test in motion and demonstrated how shifting bike positions changed the soundscape for listeners along the parade.
