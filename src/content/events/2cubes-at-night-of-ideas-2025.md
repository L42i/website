# 2CUBES at Night of Ideas

**March 28, 2025** · The Goat Farm, Atlanta, Georgia
*Performance*

2CUBES brought augmented-reality performance to Atlanta’s Night of Ideas at The Goat Farm. The work connects double bass, modular synthesizer, and two AR interfaces, allowing the performers to manipulate and spatialize their own sound through a shared virtual scene.
