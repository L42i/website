# BIKES Sound-Based Public Art Experience and Group Ride

**October 5, 2025** · Underground Atlanta and a route through Downtown and Midtown Atlanta
*Talk / public group ride / mobile sound installation*

![](/assets/events/bikes-ride-2025/1.jpg)

L42i presented a public BIKES program beginning with a talk and discussion at Underground Atlanta, followed by a group ride through Downtown and Midtown. The route connected bridges, underpasses, tunnels, public artworks, and parks as changing acoustic environments for the four-bike networked sound system. The event was organized with community cycling partners and was listed as part of Biketober.
