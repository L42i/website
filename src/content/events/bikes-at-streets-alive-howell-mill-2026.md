# BIKES at Atlanta Streets Alive: Howell Mill

**November 8, 2026** · Atlanta, Georgia
*Atlanta Streets Alive: Howell Mill, Westside Stride Edition*

We had the first intervention with the new concept "BIKES: Flocking" at the Westside Stride on November 8. The event took place on Howell Mill Road between 8th and 17th Street. More about the [event](https://www.atlantastreetsalive.org/howell-mill-westside-stride).
