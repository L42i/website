Couch 204 is L42i's main lab space, combining workstations with a flexible
setup for spatial audio and motion tracking. It is where most of the everyday
work of the lab happens: graduate research, project development, and the first
tests of installations that later move into the [3DBox](/3dbox).

## Setup and Hardware


- 16 channel audio dome with Neumann KH120 loudspeakers
- 6 graduate desks
- Project tables for hardware work and group sessions
- 4 Intel RealSense cameras for motion tracking
