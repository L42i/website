# Undergrad Research Opportunities

- Undergrad: [Undergraduate Research - 91958 - MUSI 4699 - VC](https://oscar.gatech.edu/bprod/bwckschd.p_disp_detail_sched?term_in=202608&crn_in=91958)
- Undergrad: [Music Tech Capstone I - 90502 - MUSI 4705 - VC](https://oscar.gatech.edu/bprod/bwckschd.p_disp_detail_sched?term_in=202608&crn_in=90502)
- **Times: Flexible**

**Undergraduate students can work with the lab outside the regular course schedule**: Both registrations are flexible in scope and can be arranged individually, whether as a first involvement with an ongoing project or as a final capstone.

Work usually connects to one of the current [projects](/projects) and takes place in [Couch 204](/couch204) and the [3DBox](/3dbox).

- **Undergraduate Research (MUSI 4699)** — 1 to 12 credit hours, for independent work on a lab project.
- **Music Tech Capstone I (MUSI 4705)** — the capstone registration for Music Technology students.

Contact [Henrik von Coler](/people/henrik-von-coler) to discuss a topic before registering.
