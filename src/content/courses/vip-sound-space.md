**Interacting with Sound and Space is a Vertically Integrated Project team**: Students join across class years and stay with the team over multiple semesters, taking on progressively larger parts of the work on interactive and immersive audio systems. Projects run in the [3DBox](/3dbox) and connect to the ongoing research of the lab, from spatial sound systems to networked and mobile instruments.

## Topics include:
- Spatial audio systems and Ambisonics
- Interaction design for sound and music
- Sensors, mapping and real-time control
- Building and documenting installations
