**The Technology Ensemble is dedicated to experimental scores**: Over the semester we work through a repertoire of open-form, graphic and text-based works, developing our own realizations with electronic and hybrid instruments and building the technical setups they require.

## Topics include:
- Reading and realizing graphic and text scores
- Live electronics and hybrid instrument setups
- Ensemble coordination and cueing with networked tools
- Rehearsal practice and public performance
